Compact context automatically
Before a message is sent, when the context reaches the threshold below, or leaves less than the room kept for the next response (below). The thinking of earlier responses is left out first; the conversation is summarized when that is not enough.
Continue a cut response automatically
When a response fills the context after writing some text, its thinking is left out and it goes on in its bubble, when that frees room.
Thinking included: fewer cut responses, more summaries. Text only: the thinking adapts to the room left, and the conversation is summarized only when its text no longer fits.
